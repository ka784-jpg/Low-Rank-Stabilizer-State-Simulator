# Published figure previews

Figures 1-19 are from [*Classical Simulations of Low Magic Quantum Dynamics*, arXiv:2508.20252v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.20252), by Kemal Aziz, Haining Pan, Michael J. Gullans, and J. H. Pixley. The paper and these figures are licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). They are **not** covered by the repository's MIT software license.

These PNGs were rendered at 200 dpi from the individual one-page PDF figure files in the [arXiv v3 TeX source](https://arxiv.org/src/2508.20252v3). The only change was PDF-to-PNG rasterization; no data or labels were altered. The original source filename and paper figure number are mapped below. For each figure's relationship to executable code, see the [figure-to-code map](../FIGURE_MAP.md) and the [visual code guide](../../scripts/README.md).
